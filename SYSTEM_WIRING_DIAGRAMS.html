ESP32-C3 SuperMini + INMP441 + LX-LCBST + switch + battery-sense divider + 2 LEDs

Verified on the live system: mic wiring (3V3 / GND / L/R→GND, GPIO4 SCK, GPIO5 WS, GPIO6 SD) and the node firmware. GPIO1 stays spare.
Proposed, needs bench validation: the whole battery chain, the divider and the LEDs. The divider reads about 13 uA. RED LED = SOC (off / slow / fast), BLUE LED = link (ACK-driven). Firmware for the divider and the LEDs does not exist yet - branch work.
Every ⏑ symbol is the same net: the module's VO- (battery negative through the module's protection FET). Wires join only where a dot is drawn. Pin names, not header positions. Repeat identically for nodes 2 and 3.

3 / Main hub - complete wiring (x1)

ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1 N16R8 + 2.8" ILI9341 + LX-LCBST + switch + battery-sense divider + 2 LEDs

Verified on the live system: TFT signals (CS 10, MOSI 11, SCK 12, DC 13, RST 14, BL 4, VCC 3V3, GND) and the hub firmware.
Proposed, needs bench validation: the battery chain, the divider on GPIO1 (ADC1_CH0), BLUE LED on GPIO2 (BLE client connected) and RED LED on GPIO17 (SOC). The hub's peak current with TFT + radios is unmeasured - confirm the LX-LCBST holds 5.1 V during bursts before trusting it here.
GPIO35-37 are the Octal PSRAM bus - never wire them. The TFT wiring is the recorded working one for the existing module. Every ⏑ symbol is the same net: the module's VO-.

4 / Caregiver wearable - complete wiring (x1)

ESP32-C3 SuperMini + SSD1306 OLED + LX-LCBST + switch + battery-sense divider + BC547 haptic drive (status on OLED)

Divider confirmed: 220 kΩ from B+ and 100 kΩ to ground give 4.2 V × 100/320 = 1.31 V at the ADC (the 100 nF filters it), 13 uA of divider current; the node firmware already uses this ratio (reading × 3.2). Motor: the 10 Ω caps the stall current at 3.3 V ÷ (R + 10 Ω) - about 69 mA for a 37 Ω coin ERM, inside the BC547's 100 mA; a lower-impedance motor needs 22 Ω or a 2N2222A. GPIO7 drives the switch in 250 ms pulses. No LEDs are fitted: link status is shown on the OLED; battery display needs the divider and firmware support.
Every ⏑ symbol is the same net; wires join only where a dot is drawn. The BC547 emitter is the motor's ground, and the flyback sits across the motor with the cathode up.
