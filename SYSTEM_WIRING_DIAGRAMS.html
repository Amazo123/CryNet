2 / USB test: central hub

ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1 ↔ existing 2.8-inch ILI9341 SPI display

Existing panel wiring recorded on the project. Pin positions below are symbolic; check the labels and power requirements of your exact display.

Backlight caution: GPIO4 controlled the existing panel. Do not connect an unknown bare backlight LED to a GPIO; verify the module's current and circuitry. The older 100 Ω resistor and 5 V VCC are not universal.
The hub and node communicate by ESP-NOW; no signal wire or common ground is required between them.

Power architecture for each unit — not an exact charger wiring schematic

Each cot, hub and wearable has its own battery and local supply. Retain the verified signal wiring from pages 1–2.

Do not wire a lithium charger from this block diagram. Select and verify each real module, cell, charge current, load sharing, regulator, switch and USB backfeed behaviour first. Basic TP4056 protection boards are not automatically power-path circuits.
