ESP32-C3 SuperMini + INMP441 + LX-LCBST + switch + battery-sense divider + 2 LEDs

Verified on the live system: mic wiring (3V3 / GND / L/R→GND, GPIO4 SCK, GPIO5 WS, GPIO6 SD) and the node firmware. GPIO1 stays spare.
Proposed, needs bench validation: the whole battery chain, the divider and the LEDs. The divider reads about 13 uA. RED LED = SOC (off / slow / fast), BLUE LED = link (ACK-driven). Firmware for the divider and the LEDs does not exist yet - branch work.
Every ⏑ symbol is the same net: the module's VO- (battery negative through the module's protection FET). Wires join only where a dot is drawn. Pin names, not header positions. Repeat identically for nodes 2 and 3.

ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1 N16R8 + 2.8" ILI9341 + LX-LCBST + switch + battery-sense divider + 2 LEDs

Verified on the live system: TFT signals (CS 10, MOSI 11, SCK 12, DC 13, RST 14, BL 4, VCC 3V3, GND) and the hub firmware.
Proposed, needs bench validation: the battery chain, the divider on GPIO1 (ADC1_CH0), BLUE LED on GPIO2 (BLE client connected) and RED LED on GPIO17 (SOC). The hub's peak current with TFT + radios is unmeasured - confirm the LX-LCBST holds 5.1 V during bursts before trusting it here.
GPIO35-37 are the Octal PSRAM bus - never wire them. The TFT wiring is the recorded working one for the existing module. Every ⏑ symbol is the same net: the module's VO-.

4 / Caregiver wearable - complete wiring (x1)

ESP32-C3 SuperMini + SSD1306 OLED + LX-LCBST + switch + battery-sense divider + 2 LEDs

Proposed, nothing built: the OLED + haptic path. The OLED runs from the SuperMini 3V3 pin, never from 5 V - I2C pull-ups ride on VCC and 5 V there would push 5 V levels into the C3 GPIOs. The motor is on its own switch, drawn in full on page 5 - it is not powered from the 3V3 pin here. Divider on GPIO3; BLUE LED GPIO1 (BLE link), RED LED GPIO4 (SOC).
Every ⏑ symbol is the same net: the module's VO-. Wires join only where a dot is drawn. Pin names, not header positions. The haptic drive continues on page 5.

5 / Wearable haptic drive - BC547 low-side switch

One transistor, one coin motor, one flyback diode - no I2C driver module

Check the motor before soldering: if its stall current exceeds 100 mA, the 10 Ω resistor must grow (22 Ω for a 200 mA motor) or the BC547 must be swapped for a 2N2222A. Measure the motor's stall current with a bench supply and a meter in series - do not guess.
One buzz per cry: the alert notify characteristic carries the event id, and the sketch only fires haptic_trigger() when the alert type changes to a cry, so a louder cot joining a cry does not re-buzz.
The BC547 emitter is the motor's ground: the motor's negative terminal goes to the collector, its positive terminal to 3V3. Keep the flyback diode across the motor itself, cathode to the + terminal.
